📊 Dataset Description

The dataset used for this project focuses on analyzing the relationship between lifestyle habits and health risks across a population of individuals.

🔍 Key Characteristics:
Population size: ~190+ individuals
Data type: Cross-sectional (snapshot of individuals at a point in time)
Focus areas:
Demographics
Lifestyle behavior
Health indicators
Risk classification
📁 Main Variables:
👤 Demographics
Age (grouped into ranges like 18–28, 29–38, etc.)
Gender
🚬 Lifestyle Factors
Smoking Status (Never, Current, Former)
Smoking Duration (Years of Smoking – YOS)
Cigarettes Per Day (CPD)
🧪 Health Indicators
BMI (Body Mass Index)
Cholesterol levels
Hypertension status
⚠️ Risk Classification
Health condition grouped into categories such as:
Healthy
Damaged
🎯 Purpose of the Dataset

The dataset is designed to:

Examine how smoking behavior impacts health outcomes
Identify high-risk groups based on age and lifestyle
Support data-driven health insights and early risk detection
📊 Dashboard Description

The Lifestyle & Health Risk Dashboard is an interactive analytical tool built in Microsoft Power BI to explore the impact of lifestyle choices—particularly smoking—on health risk levels.

🎯 Dashboard Objective

To provide a visual and interactive overview of how smoking habits and demographic factors influence:

Health conditions
Risk levels
Disease indicators
🧩 Key Features
📌 1. KPI Summary Section

At the top, key metrics provide a quick overview:

Total Patients
Average Age
Average BMI

👉 These dynamically update based on user selections.

🔘 2. Health Status Toggle
Users can switch between:
Damaged
Healthy

👉 This allows focused analysis on specific patient groups.

📊 3. Smoking Behavior Analysis
a. Smoking Status Distribution
Shows proportion of:
Never smokers
Current smokers
Former smokers
b. Smoking Duration & Intake
Compares:
Years of smoking (YOS)
Cigarettes per day (CPD)
Across different age groups

👉 Helps identify long-term exposure patterns.

👥 4. Gender-Based Insights
Visualizes smoking behavior differences between:
Male
Female

👉 Highlights demographic disparities in lifestyle habits.

🧪 5. Health Risk Analysis
Displays cholesterol and hypertension risk levels
Segmented by age group:
Low
Normal
High

👉 Reveals how risk increases with age and lifestyle factors.

🫀 6. Interactive Organ-Based Navigation
Vertical slicer with icons:
Heart, Kidney, Liver, Lungs, etc.

👉 Enhances user experience by allowing intuitive filtering based on body systems.

🎮 Interactivity

The dashboard is highly interactive:

Slicers filter data dynamically
Visuals cross-filter each other
User selections update KPIs and charts in real time
🎨 Design Approach
Clean, medical-themed layout
Central anatomical visual (heart) to emphasize health focus
Balanced use of charts for both distribution and trend analysis
🧠 Key Insights Delivered

The dashboard enables users to:

Identify high-risk age groups
Understand the impact of smoking intensity and duration
Compare health outcomes across gender
Detect patterns linking lifestyle to chronic conditions
🏁 Conclusion

This dashboard transforms raw health and lifestyle data into:

actionable insights for risk assessment and preventive healthcare analysis

It demonstrates how interactive visualization can support:

Better decision-making
Early intervention strategies
Deeper understanding of health trends
